Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

Operator: Ihor Stryzhnov · Contact: thetotemofficial@gmail.com · Website: https://day-forge-app-is.netlify.app/

DayForge is a personal planner for projects, tasks, habits and tracked time. This policy explains what DayForge collects, where it is stored, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to the web application at https://day-forge-app-is.netlify.app/.

1. Summary

  • DayForge works without an account. Without signing in, your data never leaves your browser.
  • If you sign in with Google, your data is synced to a database so that your devices can share it.
  • We do not sell your data, do not show ads, and do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. Data we collect

From your Google account (only when you sign in)

  • Your email address.
  • Your name and profile picture (avatar).
  • A unique user identifier issued by our authentication provider.

We request only the basic Google scopes openid, email and profile. We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google data.

Content you create

  • Projects (called goals in the app), tasks and task templates.
  • Daily rows and the tasks planned for each day.
  • Habits and habit history.
  • Time entries and the state of an active timer.
  • Settings and preferences (theme, layout, reminders, time format and similar).

What we do not collect

DayForge contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking scripts. We do not collect your location, contacts, or microphone or camera input.

3. Where your data is stored

  • On your device. All your data is stored in the browser's local storage (localStorage) on the device you use. This works whether or not you have an account.
  • On our servers, only if you sign in. Your data is copied to a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase, Inc. on cloud infrastructure (AWS). Each row is protected by row-level security, so only your own signed-in account can read or change it.

4. Why we use your data

We use your data for one purpose: to provide the planner to you, including keeping your data in step between your own devices when you are signed in. We do not use your content to train models, to build profiles, or for any other purpose.

5. Cookies and local storage

DayForge does not set tracking cookies. It uses local storage on your device for three things: your app data, your preferences, and, when you are signed in, the session that keeps you signed in (stored under the key dayforge-auth-v1). Clearing your site data for day-forge-app-is.netlify.app removes all of it.

6. Service providers

We share data only with the following processors, and only as needed to run the service:

  • Supabase, Inc. hosts the database and handles authentication.
  • Google LLC provides the Sign in with Google service.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with other third parties for their own purposes, for advertising or for marketing.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements

DayForge's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google user data only to provide or improve the user-facing features of DayForge that you sign in for.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to others, except as necessary to provide the service, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.
  • We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements, including retargeting, personalised or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not allow humans to read Google user data, except with your consent for specific messages, for security purposes, to comply with applicable law, or where the data has been aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.

8. How long we keep data

  • Local data stays on your device until you delete it or clear your browser's site data.
  • Server data stays in your account until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Deleted projects and tasks are kept as deletion records for up to 30 days so that your other devices can apply the deletion, and are then removed.

9. Deleting your data

  • Sign out stops syncing and removes the session from this device. It does not delete your local data or your server data.
  • Delete all projects and tasks in Settings → Backup and data removes them from this device and, if you are signed in, from your account on every device. It can be undone from the undo message shortly after.
  • Delete your account and server data. DayForge does not yet have an in-app account deletion button. To delete your account and all server-side data, email thetotemofficial@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We will delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Local copy. Clearing your browser's site data for day-forge-app-is.netlify.app removes everything stored on this device.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can export all of your local data at any time as a JSON file from Settings → Backup and data → Export backup. To use any other right, contact us at the address in section 12. We will respond within the time required by law.

11. Children

DayForge is a general-purpose planner and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13 (or under 16 where that age applies to you under local law) through the sign-in feature. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account.

12. Security

Connections to DayForge and to the database use HTTPS. Sign-in uses Google with PKCE, and database access is limited to your own rows. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your device and Google account protected.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will say so in the app.

14. Contact
