FAQ

Questions people ask

Is it free?

Yes. You can start for free and you do not need an account. Signing in with Google is optional and only adds sync between your devices.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app opens and works without a connection, and your changes are saved on the device. When you are online and signed in, they sync to your other devices.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in your browser. If you sign in with Google, your projects, tasks and tracked time are also synced to a database so your devices can share them. Without signing in, nothing leaves your browser.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open DayForge in your phone's browser and install it from the browser menu (for example "Add to Home Screen"). It then opens like an app.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings, go to Backup and data and choose "Delete all projects and tasks". A short undo is available, and if you are signed in it also clears your account on every device. To delete your account and server data, email thetotemofficial@gmail.com. Clearing the site data in your browser removes the local copy.
